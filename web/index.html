    What am I looking at?

    A real Shard Manager with real worker processes on this laptop. The work is 16 shards that burn real CPU.

    Worker 1demo-worker-1

    Card = worker process

    Each card is a separate OS process. The color only tells workers apart. You can find them in top.

    Square = shard

    A piece of work. Bigger and redder means more requests/sec. HOT means a visitor heated it.

    Bar = reported load

    The load the worker reports to Shard Manager. The white mark is the average of all workers. Balanced means all bars end near the mark.

    1.80×→1.05×

    Imbalance = busiest / average

    1.00× is perfect. With load balancing OFF, Shard Manager places shards only by count, so this stays red.

    Try it

    • ON
      Load balancing Shard Manager starts moving shards by their load. Watch the meter turn green.
    • 🔥
      Heat up a shard Makes one shard 5× hotter. Shard Manager moves other shards away from its worker.
    • 💀
      Kill a worker SIGKILL, no goodbye. About 2s later its shards come back on the survivors.
    • ➕
      Add a worker Starts a new process, and Shard Manager gives it a share of the shards.
