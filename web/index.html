What you're looking at

A real Shard Manager deciding where work runs, on real processes on this laptop.

Workers
Each box is a separate OS process. You can find them in top.
Shards
The squares are 32 pieces of work. The number is requests per second, and the color goes from cool to hot.
Heartbeats
Every worker checks in with Shard Manager every 2 seconds: the ring beats and a dot travels up the wire. If a worker is silent for 4 seconds, Shard Manager gives its shards to the others.
Shard moves
When Shard Manager gives a shard to another worker, its box flashes and the shard slides over. Shard Manager only decides the owner; the work itself never passes through it.
Load imbalance
The busiest worker's load divided by the quietest worker's. 1.0× means they do the same work.
Shard Manager off
Without Shard Manager, shards are only spread by count: every worker gets the same number, however busy they are. A new worker gets its share and a dead worker's shards are spread over the others, but nobody looks at load.

Try it

  • Turn on Shard Manager and watch the imbalance drop.
  • Click a shard to make it 5× hotter.
  • Press Kill on a worker. It gets SIGKILL, and its heartbeats stop. Try it with Shard Manager off, too: its shards go to the others by count.
  • The dashed + Add worker slot starts a new process, and Shard Manager gives it a share.

Press ? or Esc to close.
